About me

Patrícia is an environmental engineer with a PhD in Geophysics who specializes in fire–climate dynamics in tropical ecosystems. With over a decade of experience at the intersection of Earth observation, climate science, ecology, and policy, she studies how climate variability and human activity shape fire regimes. Her current work focuses on identifying the climatic and anthropogenic drivers of catastrophic wildfires across Amazonian tropical landscapes.
