Hi, welcome to my website!

My name is Patrícia Silva. I’m an environmental engineer with a PhD in Geophysics, specializing in fire–climate dynamics in tropical ecosystems. I currently work as a postdoctoral researcher at Yale University, where I study the climatic conditions that lead to extreme fires and map ecosystem vulnerability in the Amazon rainforest.

More broadly, I’m interested in the climatic constraints on contemporary fire regimes and extreme fire events, and in how fire regimes may evolve under future climate scenarios. I enjoy working at the intersection of science and policy, and I strive to produce actionable insights that support decision-making, strengthen ecosystem resilience, and reduce wildfire risk.

Outside of academia, I enjoy spending time with family and friends, working out, and diving into one of my many hobbies. Lately, that’s meant reading, playing board games and tabletop RPGs, and going on hikes.
